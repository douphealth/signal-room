Transparent Zero-Proxy Telemetry

On load and on every Sync, Signalroom executes throttled parallel fetches directly from your browser to official CORS-enabled Atlassian Statuspage v2 /api/v2/summary.json endpoints with a strict 4500ms AbortController timeout and real performance.now() latency timing.

Providers that block browser CORS (such as AWS, Google Cloud, Azure, and Stripe) are never fabricated—they are clearly badged as Official Portal with verified direct links, and can be hydrated live via your own Cloudflare Worker JSON endpoint.

Technical FAQ

How does Signalroom calculate serial Composite P0 SLA?

For independent serial P0 dependencies where any single vendor outage disrupts your application, Signalroom computes A_total = Product(A_i). Five independent 99.95% vendors yield 99.7502% effective availability (~108 mins/month or 21.9 hours/year exposure).

How does Signalroom fetch live status without public CORS proxies?

Signalroom fetches official CORS-enabled Atlassian Statuspage v2 /api/v2/summary.json feeds directly from your browser with a 4500ms timeout and performance.now() timing. Non-CORS portals are badged as Official Portal or hydrated via your own Cloudflare Worker.

What does the Interactive SVG Stack Topology map show?

It renders Your Production App at the center with a live composite health ring connected by animated dashed signal lines to your watched P0, P1, and P2 vendors. Clicking any node opens its live inspection drawer, and any real or drilled outage pulses the spoke amber or rose immediately.

Can I share my Watchlist with my team without creating an account?

Yes. Click Share Stack Link to copy a stateless #stack=... URL hash containing your stack name, service IDs, and priority tiers. Opening the link in any browser loads that stack instantly.

Does Outage Drill Mode ever mix simulated alerts with real telemetry?

Never. Drills are stored in an isolated session state with a prominent DRILL MODE banner and a one-click Reset Drills button, keeping live API telemetry 100% pure.

How can I hydrate all 99 feeds live, including AWS, GCP, and Stripe?

Click the Worker API settings icon in the top command bar and connect your own Cloudflare Worker JSON endpoint (e.g., https://api.signalroom.store/status) to hydrate all 99 providers server-side with zero CORS blocks.
